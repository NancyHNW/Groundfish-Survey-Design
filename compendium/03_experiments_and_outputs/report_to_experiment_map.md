# Report-to-experiment map

The report's results section is supported by the artifact register in
[`report_output_register.csv`](report_output_register.csv).

## Key report conclusions and evidence

- The default full-survey route has a substantial probability of overflow, but
  the mean cost of uncertainty is modest relative to planned time.
- Overflows occur disproportionately late in trips and are concentrated among
  a small subset of stations and vessels.
- Planner seed variability is large enough to require repeated seeds and
  paired comparisons.
- Capacity buffers reduce overflow risk but increase planned route time.
- Recourse strategy performance depends on the policy and threshold; results
  must be compared using the same scenario design.
